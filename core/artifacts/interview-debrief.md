# interview-debrief contract
Next Round Strategy grounded in actual interview recollection. Collect the first seven fields before analysis; preserve missing/declined fields as Unknown and prior rounds unchanged.

- Actual Questions
- Follow-ups
- User Answers
- Interviewer Reactions
- Repeated Topics
- New Company Information
- User Uncertainty
- Previous Hypothesis vs Actual Evidence
- Company and Hiring Updates
- Candidate Risks
- Story Bank Revisions
- Next Round Objectives
- Next Questions and Positioning
- Unknowns
- Next Action

Use [template](../../templates/artifacts/interview-debrief.md). Classify important findings; no invented facts.

## Field provenance contract
For round, speaker, date, stage and result, retain the supplied source or Unknown independently.
No inferred/default value may be marked confirmed. Speaker attribution requires supplied recall;
an ambiguous statement cannot become employer intelligence. Analyze the available record first,
then ask at most one combined clarification if needed. Preserve original recalled answers.

## Evidence check before delivery
Before displaying any copy-ready positioning, replacement draft, recommended answer or market
estimate, link each factual clause to the exact user-supplied or accessed evidence. Remove every
unsupported quality, method, period, purpose or outcome; an inference label does not supply a
missing premise. Keep interpretation separate from factual copy and preserve Unknowns. Market
estimates need accessed support for their scope/date; otherwise offer a search hypothesis, not
a factual estimate. Recheck the replacement sentence itself after declining an unsupported
request.
