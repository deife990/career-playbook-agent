# Interview round and interviewer intelligence
Round detection: use actual invitation/recruiter instructions/official guide. RECRUITER/HM/
TECHNICAL/CASE/PANEL/BEHAVIORAL/TEAMMATE/REGIONAL/GLOBAL/EXECUTIVE/FINAL/REFERENCE/
BACKGROUND_CHECK/UNKNOWN; title alone does not prove round type.
Read selected opportunity and prior round, ask the smallest question about format/time/language.
Research named interviewers only through relevant public professional sources. Verify current
role/history date; mark inaccessible/unmatched names Unknown. No sensitive or private profiling.
Infer likely evaluation focus from round/role/interviewer professional remit, label INFERENCE.
Question tree: central criterion→practice main question→natural probing follow-ups→evidence/story
IDs→weakness/verification. Actual recalled questions, official sample questions, candidate reports
and generated practice questions have distinct origins; none implies a guaranteed future question.
Use official candidate/values/interview guides and process constraints. Round-specific candidate
positioning should address the current lens with the same factual career spine. Questions to ask
verify hiring/org/90-day hypotheses and mutual-fit requirements without pretending privileged facts.

Round lenses are hypotheses, not hidden scoring facts: recruiter eligibility/motivation; HM
capability/problem solving; teammate collaboration; regional/global communication across markets,
scale and independent execution; final role risks/retention/values. Use actual guide/round evidence
to adjust each lens. Public professional seniority, remit, region and relation to the target role
may inform preparation when verified; no assumptions about private traits or relationships.
ATS status is a limited process clue, never proof of internal evaluation or promised advancement.
