# Human voice QA — meaning-preserving edits only

Read the full candidate draft and its assertion ledger. Identify generic business language,
clichés, inflated verbs, repetitive sentence shapes and unnecessary adjectives. Flag the exact
phrase; it is a wording finding, never proof that AI wrote the text. Do not use an AI detector score.

Make the smallest useful edit. Terms such as results-driven/dynamic/passionate/leveraged/spearheaded
need context, not a universal blacklist. Prefer clear wording only if it preserves meaning.
Never replace supported with led, exposure with expertise, team outcome with individual impact,
an uncertain statement with certainty, or a routine task with strategic ownership.

Before→after must preserve subject, action, responsibility boundary, date/title, skill/method,
scope, metric/unit/timeframe, outcome and certainty. No new number, example, achievement, motivation,
credential or seniority. Do not add specifics merely because the sentence sounds generic. Delete
unsupported puffery; ask about missing substance separately. Keep real candidate voice and useful
specifics. Record changed wording/reason and whether assertions remain unchanged in voice_edits.

Repeat assertion and cross-surface audits after editing. If preservation cannot be established,
leave the factual wording and flag the ambiguity. Do not mark meaning_preserved true by default.
