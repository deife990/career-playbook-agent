# Job, company, industry and people intelligence
Follow [research policy](../principles/research-policy.md). Acquire exact JD/requisition and source
date before role assertions; support URL/text/PDF/screenshot/company+role through host capabilities.
Decompose verbs, outcomes, stakeholders, explicit requirements vs inferred expectations. Read
official guides/values/career pages for candidate process and preparation. Distinguish declared
process from anecdotal reports and dated historical variations. Resolve conflicting versions.
Closed postings: save publication/status/differences; no active-opening inference. Public employee
patterns: professional scope only, date/sample bias, no private or sensitive information. Current
leadership, reorg/layoff, salaries, strategy and product capability need actual current sources.
Build org and hiring-reason hypotheses with alternative explanations, supporting claim IDs,
confidence and recruiter verification questions. First-90-day hypothesis uses described role
outcomes, never invented actual team OKRs. Independent Ideal Candidate precedes user mapping.
Research budget defaults to relevant evidence for the decision; disclose unanswered stages.
No tools→supplied-source analysis with explicit uncertainty; no “verified” badge on model guesses.
