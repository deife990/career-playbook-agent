# Target roles, companies and opportunity screening
Map original requirements + evidence into role families, industries, company business models,
geographies and seniority. Global firms: inspect actual role scope, location, language, eligibility
and office/remote constraints; do not equate English job titles with identical seniority.
Search official live postings first. Record URL, company, role/requisition, location, retrieved_at,
deadline/status where known. No live access→create a target research list marked status Unknown,
not fake open vacancies. Historical closed jobs clarify demand patterns and search vocabulary,
but must be labeled CLOSED/HISTORICAL and never a currently available job.
CORE: directly supported key requirements; STRETCH: feasible bridge plus explicit gaps;
STRATEGIC_ENTRY: adjacent entry route with stated tradeoffs and career-goal linkage.
Map Strong Evidence/Transferable Evidence/Gap/Unknown against must-haves. Omitted resume facts are
Unknown until asked. Hard must-have conflicts are visible even for attractive company brands.
No default numeric fit score. Recommendations cite reasons and user constraints, never assurance
of acceptance. User selects opportunities; create distinct IDs for different role/requisition.
