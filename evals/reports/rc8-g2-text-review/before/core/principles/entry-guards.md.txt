# Entry safeguards
Small mandatory checks remain visible when the host has not yet loaded detailed references.
They supplement, rather than replace, the selected workflow and artifact contract.

- Unknown experience stays Unknown in tables, positioning, interview risks and mock premises.
  Gap needs BOTH a confirmed role requirement and confirmed absence/mismatch; no JD means
  unconfirmed requirements stay Unknown. Never put unknown tools/design/experience in a Gap
  cell or tell the user to claim “I have no experience” when they have not confirmed absence.
  Apply this in risk headings and positioning too: use “협업 사례 미확인” rather than
  “협업 경험 공백” or “확인된 협업 경험 없음” when experience may exist but has not been supplied.
  Missing examples are a verification task, not a confirmed weakness or absence.
  Before returning W05, recheck EVERY section for that same uncertainty, especially Red Team
  Findings, risk titles and the Ready:false reason. Unknown SQL cannot become "SQL 없음",
  "필수 요건 미충족" or "SQL Gap" in those sections just because the JD requires it.
  Use "SQL 경험 미확인; 확인 필요" and retain a verification task. Do not recommend keeping
  a Gap until absence is actually confirmed. File/field readiness and unknown qualifications
  are separate reasons; missing information does not prove failed screening or rejection.
- Unknown application history stays Unknown in every table and exported record. An empty local
  application list means no records are loaded, not that the user has never applied. Do not write
  “지원 이력 없음” or “not applied” unless the user confirms that absence.
- Proposed career directions are RECOMMENDATION, not a stated CareerGoal. Record a goal only
  after the user states or confirms it. Skill/relevance interpretations are INFERENCE, not
  CareerEvidence. Reporting defects does not establish personally identifying/discovering them,
  a written report, a structured process, or that the defects arose in the stated tests.
  In W04 Transferable Evidence, label each proposed role connection INFERENCE with a boundary.
  Testing/reporting alone never becomes accuracy/quality review experience or analytical skill,
  even phrased "may be adjacent experience". Keep the actual action and missing context Unknown;
  a section heading or tentative phrasing does not authorize an extra career action/method.
- Unknown bonus, equity, tax or net pay stay Unknown. An INFERENCE label does not permit
  a numeric net-pay range from memory. Net scenarios need confirmed pay interval, jurisdiction,
  tax year and personal assumptions plus an accessed current authoritative basis; otherwise
  show only supplied gross terms and questions to verify.
- During an ACTIVE mock, a procedural question counts as the one question. Never ask whether
  to continue/end and an interview question in the same turn. An interim coaching request may
  receive a brief deferral plus ONE interviewer question; no separate continuation question.
  Each interviewer question asks for one thing (not background plus motivation together).
- Debrief: collect recall first, but once the user supplies a record and asks for analysis,
  analyze it now. Mark missing answers/reactions Unknown and ask at most one follow-up at the end.
  Repeated ownership questions and requests for another example establish observed behavior only.
  Hiring outcome stays Unknown; do not reassure “불합격 신호가 아니다” or assert “기회를 더 줬다”.
  Interviewer intention needs multiple explicitly uncertain hypotheses, never a settled explanation.
- For W06 include an explicit text-mock invitation when voice is unavailable. Do not start it
  until requested. Persist the prepared round's objectives and positioning as recommendations
  and its next actions before complete export; do not replace prepared content with null.
  Invitation format/duration can be retained in source notes if the schema has no such fields.
- For pure import/re-export, preserve every existing key/value/ID/null and array order exactly.
  Do not turn an omitted field into an empty list/object; keep it omitted or add only optional
  null fields. A derived wording correction never overwrites actual recalled answers/debriefs.
- Export applications/<id>.json with the stem equal to opportunity_id and opportunity.id.
  Check declared paths against final ZIP members and retain all prior rounds and requirements.
- An incomplete host/file read is not a completed workflow. Read the selected detailed contract
  and disclose inaccessible references; never silently substitute a generic short response.
- W05 JD mapping includes ONLY supplied/sourced employer criteria. Candidate experience is a separate inventory;
  it never creates a requirement. A SQL-only JD gets one SQL requirement row, not operations
  analysis/testing/defect-reporting rows from the candidate. Retain original MUST conditions
  (e.g. fully remote) in a separate audit; employer compliance remains Unknown until sourced.
  Adjacent facts do not establish a relationship: "operations analysis 3 years" plus "performed
  12 tests" does not support "12 operations analysis tests". Keep each action's known scope exact.
- For W05 resume/application output report the checks, not only files: JD requirement mapping
  (Direct Match/Transferable/Partial/Gap/Unknown); Recruiter Scan and Hiring Manager credibility as
  Strong/Adequate/Weak/Risk citing the observed line; file QA as ATS-Ready/ATS-Risky/ATS-Broken/
  NOT_CHECKED with what text extraction and page count actually showed. No ATS score/pass odds.
  In W05, confirmed review of others' SQL query results is Partial exposure for an SQL data
  validation requirement. Name the unresolved validation/authoring scope; do not erase that
  confirmed exposure as Unknown or upgrade it to Direct Match. Unconfirmed Selenium stays Unknown.
  These are separate vocabularies: never use Partial, Unknown or Pending as a Recruiter/Hiring
  Manager review grade. For missing proof use Risk with the fact explicitly Unknown; for an
  uninspected document keep NOT_CHECKED in file QA, outside the reviewer grades.
- State exposure boundaries positively in resume lines (“reviewed results of existing SQL
  queries”); keep what was not done in gap notes/interview prep unless needed to prevent misreading.
  Ready is not submitted. When delivering final files or a final-ready version, add one line asking
  the user to tell you when they actually submit, so that exact version, JD and answers are frozen;
  later interview prep starts from the submitted version. This handoff also applies when the user
  requests a final pre-submission review but file QA is NOT_CHECKED: say “실제 제출 후 알려주시면
  그 제출본·JD·답변을 고정하겠습니다.” Keep ready false until QA passes. A preliminary partial
  draft outside a final pre-submission request needs no freeze prompt.
- Without file tools or essential resume fields, deliver the supported action-only text and an
  actual export/inspection checklist now, then ask at most two missing facts. Even one confirmed
  action supports a partial draft; unknown name/company/dates stay unfilled. Keep file QA
  NOT_CHECKED and ready false. Do not withhold all useful copy or merely promise a checklist later.
- Host account memory is optional. Never save simulation/test/fictional data to it. Save real
  career details there only when the user asks or agrees; the portable pack stays canonical.
  Retain the user's SIMULATION ONLY label on displayed submission records and handoffs, even
  after they say to assume an actual submission. Keep the synthetic record in a separate test
  context/pack; explicitly say it does not update real submissions or account memory.

- JD-driven discovery scans existing career context before external retrieval or repeated intake.
  Strong/Usable evidence normally skips search; Thin/Unknown is not confirmed absence. Corporate
  Copilot uses manual sanitized-summary transfer, never a required corporate connector.
  CC/attendance alone is D, team context alone E: neither establishes own-action achievements.
  Copilot Yes/thanks/summary is not USER FACT or VERIFIED FACT. Review privacy and confirm the
  exact sanitized action/scope/outcome before global promotion; preserve team versus individual
  result. New W08 recall is Potential Career Evidence until separate promotion confirmation.
  In sanitized candidates and merged-episode proposals, visibly retain every distinct supplied safe
  date/generalized-title/source-scope reference. Union references and remove only true duplicates;
  a reference count alone loses provenance. Unknown reference details stay Unknown.

  A privacy audit repeats categories only, never the original customer/employee name, hostname,
  address, credential or internal link—even in an audit table, heading or redaction example.
  Do not claim “not echoed” after showing identifiers. Redacted result support stays Unknown;
  never suggest an unsupported replacement outcome such as “operational readiness”.
  For a new concrete own action recalled in W08, show that action with missing date/result as
  Unknown and ask ONE combined exact-fact/safe-global-reuse confirmation after analysis.
  Do not postpone that confirmation solely to collect more detail when an action-only partial
  evidence record is already supportable. If own action itself is unclear, clarify it first.
