# Application Pack contract
Include the requested components and a visible SKIP/DEFER decision for optional work; do not
force a portfolio, cover letter or public profile rewrite where not needed.
- Positioning and Evidence
- Master Resume
- Tailored Resume and Change Log
- LinkedIn Audit
- Digital Footprint
- Portfolio or Handout Decision
- Cover Letter
- Application Answers
- Recruiter and Networking Drafts
- Red Team Findings
- Consistency Audit
- Unknowns and Final Checks
- Next Action

Every final resume bullet/story/answer traces to confirmed evidence_ids; maintain dates/titles/
ownership/metrics consistency. Include change log and red-team risk/severity/evidence/fix.
Unknowns and unresolved factual contradictions block final-ready status. No placeholders are
presented as real facts. “Ready” never implies application submission. Use
[template](../../templates/artifacts/application-pack.md).

## v1.1 screening deliverables
Use [screening engine](../capabilities/screening-engine.md) and [artifact QA](../capabilities/resume-artifact.md).
Include requested mode/depth, selection strategy and JD category/Direct Match/Transferable/Partial/
Gap/Unknown coverage table with evidence and missing criteria. Separate ATS structural result,
Recruiter Scan and HM Credibility judgments, differentiation anchors, concern-to-surface plan,
meaning-preserving edits and cross-surface findings. Each judgment cites a line/section/fact.
Recruiter Scan and HM Credibility grades are exactly Strong/Adequate/Weak/Risk with a rationale.
Missing proof may receive Risk while the underlying fact stays Unknown. Match classifications
Direct Match/Transferable/Partial/Gap/Unknown and file QA ATS-Ready/ATS-Risky/ATS-Broken/NOT_CHECKED
are separate fields, never substitutes for either reviewer grade.
Include actual DOCX/PDF links only when created, observed extraction/page/visual checks and pending
checks separately. Final-ready requires complete relevant checks, not a percentage score.
Show submitted version/date/JD/answer identity after confirmed submission and the interview handoff.
Use [submission template](../../templates/artifacts/resume-submission.md).

## Evidence check before delivery
Before displaying any copy-ready positioning, replacement draft, recommended answer or market
estimate, link each factual clause to the exact user-supplied or accessed evidence. Remove every
unsupported quality, method, period, purpose or outcome; an inference label does not supply a
missing premise. Keep interpretation separate from factual copy and preserve Unknowns. Market
estimates need accessed support for their scope/date; otherwise offer a search hypothesis, not
a factual estimate. Recheck the replacement sentence itself after declining an unsupported
request.
