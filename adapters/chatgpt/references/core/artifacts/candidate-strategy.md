# Candidate Strategy Brief contract
Required sections, in order:
Keep every named section, including for a short brief or a second company. A one-line Unknown
entry is sufficient; do not merge or omit Unknowns, Confirmed Context, risks or Sources.
- Executive Summary
- What This Role Appears To Be
- Why It May Be Open
- Confirmed Context
- Hiring Hypothesis
- Ideal Candidate
- Your Strong Evidence
- Transferable Evidence
- Gaps
- Unknowns
- Candidate Positioning
- Likely Interview Risks
- Questions To Verify
- Recommended Next Action
- Sources

Executive Summary: 3–5 useful conclusions with classifications and one action.
Role/business interpretation must precede candidate personalization. Why open/hiring/90-day/org
are INFERENCE unless directly sourced. Ideal Candidate is independently derived from JD/company.
This is the reasoning sequence, not an instruction to reorder the fifteen output sections:
the Executive Summary can summarize the completed mapping, and Confirmed Context can list
user-provided facts. Neither is evidence that the ideal persona was anchored to the user.
Four evidence buckets require criterion→actual evidence IDs→reason; resume omissions are Unknown,
not presumed Gap. Positioning has a truthful evidence spine and a acknowledged boundary.
Risks contain skeptical questions plus evidence-backed response direction, not fabricated answers.
Risk titles must preserve Unknown too. Unprovided campaign/collaboration examples are “사례 미확인”,
not “경험 공백” or confirmed absence. Positioning cannot turn an inferred transferable connection
into a factual skill, such as anomaly detection or structured reporting, without user evidence.
Each proposed transferable connection carries its own INFERENCE label and a specific boundary.
"Performed tests and reported defects" is not "accuracy/quality review experience", even phrased
as "may be adjacent experience"; that adds an unknown action or method. Keep the confirmed action
and say the connection to campaign analysis is Unknown pending actual context. Never use a
Transferable Evidence heading alone as an implicit provenance label.
Sources: title, URL, publisher, published/retrieved date, source type, freshness, confidence and
associated claim/classification. No fake citation. Missing sources appear in Unknowns.
No numerical fit score, probability of hiring or uncited internal facts. Use
[template](../../templates/artifacts/candidate-strategy.md). Sources remain opportunity-scoped.

## Evidence check before delivery
Before displaying any copy-ready positioning, replacement draft, recommended answer or market
estimate, link each factual clause to the exact user-supplied or accessed evidence. Remove every
unsupported quality, method, period, purpose or outcome; an inference label does not supply a
missing premise. Keep interpretation separate from factual copy and preserve Unknowns. Market
estimates need accessed support for their scope/date; otherwise offer a search hypothesis, not
a factual estimate. When at least one own action is confirmed, deliver a usable action-only
sentence now. Missing outcomes or metadata must not postpone that supported sentence. Recheck
the replacement sentence itself after declining an unsupported request.
