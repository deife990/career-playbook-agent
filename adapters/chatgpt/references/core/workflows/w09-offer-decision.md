# W09 Offer & Decision
## Purpose
Evaluate a real offer against the original reasons for changing jobs and support a human decision.
## Trigger
“오퍼 받았어”, compare offers, negotiation, compensation/reference/background-check questions.
## Required context
Read [policies](../principles/index.md), [state](../state/state.md), original requirements/goals,
selected opportunity and actual offer summary. Multiple companies→clarify; compare distinct scoped
offers in a deliberate matrix, not a merged company record. If original requirements are missing,
ask what mattered at search start; do not create a convenient baseline to favor this offer.
## Steps
1. Confirm selected company/role and actual terms/deadline; ask only the most decision-critical
   missing items. No full confidential contract requested; sanitized terms are sufficient.
2. Use [compensation](../capabilities/compensation.md) to separate guaranteed base from bonus,
   sign-on contingencies, equity, benefits and gross/net assumptions. Current finance/legal sources
   are needed for claims outside supplied terms; unavailable estimates stay Unknown.
3. Compare each original MUST/PREFER/AVOID criterion with MET/NOT_MET/UNKNOWN and factual reason.
   Highlight role/scope, manager/team, growth, location/style, stability and workload risks.
4. Examine career direction vs immediate rewards; present tradeoffs, alternatives and uncertainty.
5. Prepare reference/background-check consistency and privacy checklist before any contact.
6. Develop evidence-backed negotiation alternatives; [messages](../capabilities/networking.md)
   remain drafts, no fake competing offer or salary. Respect confirmed timing, not invented urgency.
7. Output [Offer Decision Brief](../artifacts/offer-brief.md) with ACCEPT/DECLINE/NEGOTIATE/DEFER
   alternatives, questions-to-verify and one recommendation grounded in original constraints.
8. User decides. Update [pipeline](../capabilities/application-pipeline.md) only on actual confirmed
   action/event; acceptance recommendation alone does not set ACCEPTED.
## Evidence rules
Supplied terms USER FACT unless verified independently; uncertain values/scenarios INFERENCE;
suggested action RECOMMENDATION. No default weighted/numeric fit score or return guarantee.
## Output
Offer Decision Brief and negotiation/reference/background-check drafts where requested.
## State updates
CompensationPackage/Offer (original_requirement_ids)/Decision/NextAction scoped to company; real
offer event may set OFFER with history. ACCEPTED/DECLINED only on explicit confirmed user action.
Preserve original requirements and offer terms; later amendments are versions, not silent overwrites.
## Fallbacks
No terms→decision checklist with unknowns. No original requirements→reconstruct and confirm. No
market/legal access→known gross terms and authoritative verification questions, no invented laws.
## Do not
Choose for the user, overwrite must-haves for salary, infer tax/vesting/manager quality, contact
references or accept/decline/send negotiation without authorization.
