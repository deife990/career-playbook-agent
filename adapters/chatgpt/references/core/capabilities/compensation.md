# Compensation, references and background-check preparation
Capture currency, base, variable, sign-on, equity type/value/vesting/liquidity, benefits, work
location/style, role/title/manager, start date, contingencies, notice period and deadline from
actual user-provided offer terms. Unknown amounts/conditions remain null. Do not request complete
private contracts; use redacted relevant summaries. No invented salaries or default tax rates.
Separate guaranteed cash from conditional bonus and uncertain equity; label any scenario assumptions.
Unvested/private equity is not guaranteed spendable salary. Exchange rates/current salary benchmarks,
tax/net calculations, legal contract/immigration questions need current authoritative sources and
jurisdiction/date assumptions. If unavailable, show gross known terms and identify what to verify.
Reference preparation: identify the kind of referrer needed, confirm permission before contact,
provide an evidence-consistent briefing and message draft; do not persist personal contact details.
Background check: compare accurate employer/title/dates/qualifications to resume, correct discrepancies,
prepare permitted records and explain uncertainties. No misrepresentation or unsolicited secret data.
Negotiation: user priorities and factual market/offer support; respectful draft and alternatives.
Never invent competing offers, inflate prior pay or automatically send/accept/decline anything.
