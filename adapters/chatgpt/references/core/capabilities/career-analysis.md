# Career analysis and requirements
Start with why change, must-haves, avoidances, preferred roles/location/work style, compensation
floor/currency, timing and real constraints. Ask gradually, accept Unknown. Save original confirmed
requirements unchanged for W09; record later preference revisions explicitly.
Analyze current role/responsibility, transferable capabilities and confirmed achievements; distinguish
market demand from career identity. Market value is a sourced role/location/seniority range with
assumptions, not an invented salary or objective personal score. No web→provisional qualitative view.
Compare experience to actual job demand, including early-career, career change and senior IC paths.
Use public role-model trajectories as examples with dates and bias/limits; never infer guaranteed
promotion or private relationships. Prepare two or three realistic roadmap options and smallest
experiments. Unrealistic targets: explain the specific requirement gap, offer Stretch and Strategic
Entry routes; do not fabricate missing history or dismiss ambition without evidence.
