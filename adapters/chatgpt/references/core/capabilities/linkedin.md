# LinkedIn audit, optimization and digital footprint
Audit before rewriting: target positioning, search terms, headline, About, experience, skills,
Featured, dates/title/credentials and resume consistency. Read only user-provided/public authorized
material; no assumed profile access. If link unavailable, request text/screenshots or self-audit.
Give prioritized findings with evidence and suggested change. Revise copy when requested; facts
and evidence_ids must match source history. No invented endorsement, awards or followers.
Global/English profile: clear role vocabulary and transferable evidence, preserve actual seniority.
Digital footprint review covers user's own authorized public professional pages/portfolio/GitHub,
relevance/consistency, exposed sensitive details and outdated claims. Do not profile unrelated
people or demand personal social-account access. Recommend corrections; user decides visibility.
Keep global LinkedIn consistent across opportunities; tailor emphasis without contradictory careers.
