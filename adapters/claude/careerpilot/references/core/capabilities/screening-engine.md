# Evidence-backed Screening Optimization Engine

Use inside W05 with [resume](resume.md), [forms](application-writing.md), [document QA](resume-artifact.md)
and selected Application state. Career facts/evidence outrank old resume wording and every external
framework. One shared workflow, no additional skill installation. No probability or numeric fit score.

## Intake and modes

Resolve target opportunity before company-specific work. Ask only the one/two missing facts needed
next: target role/market/language, existing copy or evidence, deadline/official field constraints.
Collect role/domain/business context, personal responsibility, audience/stakeholders and actual
change. Do not ask for confidential systems, customer identities or unredacted contracts.

Choose from the request, never require command syntax:
- BUILD_MASTER: comprehensive global evidence inventory; target-independent, not necessarily short.
- AUDIT_EXISTING: inspect supplied copy before proposing changes; unsupported old claims need review.
- TAILOR_TO_JOB: derive selected opportunity version from Master and W04 analysis.
- FINAL_SUBMISSION: finish all factual/structural/human/document checks and present actual files.

Depth QUICK shows top three supported findings; STANDARD covers relevant roles and all dimensions;
DEEP covers full history/transition and drill-down. Depth changes presentation and effort, never
evidence checks. Unperformed steps remain PENDING, not PASS. A quick audit is not final-ready.

## Shared pipeline and checkpoints

1. **Career Evidence**: use confirmed evidence and story facts. Identify contradictory/unknown fields.
2. **Candidate Positioning**: use W04 thesis and real repeated strengths. Global Master may use target
   role goals without pretending that a company-specific strategy already exists.
3. **Master Resume**: inventory true roles, actual titles/dates, education/certifications/projects,
   skills and candidate bullets. Keep assertions/provenance for summary and sections as well as bullets.
4. **Target Job Analysis**: reuse W04 JD, version/date/source. Requirements have stable keys and
   categories REQUIRED/PREFERRED/RESPONSIBILITIES/DOMAIN/TOOLS_TECHNOLOGIES/STAKEHOLDERS/SENIORITY/
   BUSINESS_OUTCOMES/HIDDEN_INFERRED. Hidden needs always INFERENCE with confidence and verification
   question. Frequency/order are clues, not proof of hard screening rules. Omission is not absence.
5. **Resume Selection Strategy**: record what to emphasize/retain/condense/remove and why. Preserve
   clear chronology; prioritize relevant bullets within roles or use an explicitly labeled relevant
   experience section. Never conceal gaps or make previous employment look current.
6. **Resume Tailoring**: reorder real experience/skills, explain facts more clearly, use accurate
   industry terms and a supported summary. Do not change titles, years, ownership, metrics or tools.
7. **Evidence Assertion Audit**: split every sentence into action/subject/ownership/scope/method/
   outcome; trace each part to a confirmed fact, story and source field. Remove unsupported parts
   before presenting drafts. Unknown benefit means no result clause. A source containing a number
   somewhere does not prove that a proposed outcome uses that number correctly.
8. **ATS Structural QA**: inspect layout/fields using [document QA](resume-artifact.md). No extraction
   capability means NOT_CHECKED, not ATS-Ready. Formatting risk is separate from qualification gaps.
9. **JD Coverage Analysis**: each requirement maps DIRECT_MATCH (same demonstrated work), TRANSFERABLE
   (different setting with explicit bridge), PARTIAL (some criteria met; name remainder), GAP (confirmed
   missing experience), UNKNOWN (not enough facts). Store evidence/story IDs and selected bullet keys.
   Do not turn Unknown into Gap or a missing JD keyword into a user's skill. Hidden needs stay hypotheses.
10. **Recruiter Scan Test**: simulate a short first look (5–10 seconds is a review exercise, not a
    universal measured statistic). State who this candidate appears to be, target role clarity,
    recent title/company/scope/date visibility, top evidence placement, density, hierarchy,
    progression, generic phrasing, immediate concerns and reason to keep reading. For each finding
    use Strong/Adequate/Weak/Risk plus observable line/section and rationale. No 87/100 scores.
11. **Hiring Manager Credibility**: could the candidate perform the work? Probe exposure vs ownership,
    seniority boundary, team vs personal outcome, causal evidence, technical/business specificity,
    defensible interview drill-down and why to interview. Label reviewer judgments INFERENCE.
12. **Differentiation Audit**: find repeated demonstrated strengths, unusual combinations, business
    exposure, environments, cross-functional patterns and career narrative. State evidence anchors.
    Remove interchangeable traits; do not invent a unique insight or require a provocative opinion.
13. **Human Voice**: [meaning-preserving edit](human-voice.md), then repeat assertion audit. A stronger
    verb is not an improvement if it changes responsibility. Keep uncertainty and boundaries.
14. **Cross-Surface Consistency**: compare selected resume, Master facts, LinkedIn, portfolio,
    form answers and interview pitch on titles/dates/skills/metrics/ownership. Different emphasis
    is fine; contradictory facts block final status. Missing surfaces remain Unknown, not aligned.
15. **DOCX/PDF Rendering** and 16. **Text Extraction/Parse QA**: follow [artifact contract](resume-artifact.md).
17. **Final Red Team**: recruiter→HM→skeptical interviewer, evidence/date/company checks again.
18. **Freeze Submitted Version**: ready is not submitted. After actual user-confirmed submission,
    freeze the exact version, JD identity/text/sources, date, bullet keys/summary, answers/cover
    letter and artifact paths/hashes. Preserve the linked ResumeVersion without changes. New
    correction/resubmission needs a new version/submission ID. See [state](../state/state.md).

Record stage status and observations/fixes in Application.screening. ResumeVersion holds its own
claim ledger and immutable factual content. Pending user facts block affected drafts/finalization;
deliver supported components, a short remaining checklist and one next action. Re-run checks
affected by later edits, especially extraction after rerendering. Never claim a real ATS portal
test from local extraction alone.

## Bullet engine and Story Bank pipeline

Use Action + actual change + confirmed scope/context + useful method + verified outcome when
available. XYZ/STAR/CAR are optional structures. No mandatory metric. Unknown outcome/metric stays
unknown; confirmed frequency/complexity/geography/stakeholders/systems/responsibility may provide
context without invented benefits. Keep supported/used/worked with for exposure/contribution;
owned/led/architected requires explicit matching responsibility evidence.

Search confirmed stories for relevant achievements missing from Master. Use Situation/Task/Action/
Result/Skills/Scope/Evidence/strength assessment; propose a concise candidate bullet and link story
and evidence IDs. Do not compress a whole spoken story into one line or treat practice answers as
career evidence. A story's skill tag alone is not proof. Preserve version history through immutable
ResumeVersion IDs, revision/derived_from_id and previous_text, submitted records and actual round IDs.

## Concern management

Identify possible concerns from actual chronology/JD/history: short tenure, gap, change of function,
title/seniority/industry/geography mismatch, required skill and unusual path. Reviewer concern is
an INFERENCE, not a verdict or an invented explanation. Ask for actual reasons when needed. Decide
the surface: resume for factual clarity; application if asked; recruiter screen for transition;
interview for nuanced evidence/learning. Do not invent an intentional move, layoff or growth story.
Preserve privacy, avoid defensive overexplaining and do not hide dates or missing qualifications.
