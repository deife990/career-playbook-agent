# W02 Career Strategy
## Purpose
Turn current evidence and original requirements into realistic career options and a roadmap.
## Trigger
Long-term direction, career change, role model, career roadmap or “어떤 직무가 좋을까”.
## Required context
Read [policies](../principles/index.md), [state](../state/state.md), Career Brief and goals.
If the profile is incomplete, ask only the experience/constraint that changes the recommendation.
## Steps
1. Confirm horizon and decision requirements using [career analysis](../capabilities/career-analysis.md).
2. Assess current position, career capital and transferable skills with evidence links.
3. Research current demand/value using comparable role/location/seniority; label uncertainty and
   salary assumptions. No web means no current numerical salary assertion.
4. Compare two or three directions: fit to requirements, direct/transferable evidence, gaps,
   entry routes, opportunity cost and uncertainty. Include a realistic option when targets stretch.
5. Research public role-model career patterns, attribute sources/dates; avoid success-story bias
   and never make a reported public trajectory a guaranteed template.
6. Use [role mapping](../capabilities/role-mapping.md) for Core/Stretch/Strategic Entry routes.
7. Draft 30/60/90-day experiments with milestones, evidence to collect, user effort and review dates.
8. Produce [Career Roadmap](../artifacts/career-roadmap.md); ask which direction to pursue, then W03.
## Evidence rules
Market observations need sources, pathways are INFERENCE, proposed experiments RECOMMENDATION.
Do not treat a target role's requirements as the user's existing competencies.
## Output
Career Roadmap with options/decision criteria/gaps/sources and one next experiment.
## State updates
CareerGoal/TargetRole/Skill/Decision/NextAction. Keep original requirements intact; record explicit
changes rather than silently replacing them. No automatic opportunity or offer acceptance.
## Fallbacks
No web: provisional options and source checklist. No direction: two modest experiments. No profile:
partial roadmap with Unknowns; do not invent a complete career.
## Do not
Use brand prestige alone, invent market value or present one best path with false certainty.
