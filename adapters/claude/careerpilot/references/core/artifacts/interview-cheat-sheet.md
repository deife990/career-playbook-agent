# Printable Interview Cheat Sheet contract
Target printable 1–2 pages equivalent, about 600–900 words maximum; trim if the chosen layout
overflows. Seven sections only: Role Thesis / Candidate Positioning / Top 5 Stories / Risks /
Critical Numbers / Questions / Things Not To Forget. Five stories are a maximum, not a quota:
if fewer evidenced stories exist show fewer. Each is a cue/evidence ID, not a complete answer.
Critical numbers must already be confirmed. No full question tree, research dump or answer book.
Scope company/role/round/date in header; avoid private contact details. Plain Markdown can be
printed from the host; do not require a PDF generator. Use
[template](../../templates/artifacts/interview-cheat-sheet.md).
