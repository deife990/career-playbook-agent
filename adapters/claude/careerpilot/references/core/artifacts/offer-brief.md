# Offer Decision Brief contract
Compare every original MUST/PREFER/AVOID requirement against actual offer terms as MET / NOT_MET /
UNKNOWN. A high salary cannot silently override an original must-have. Include:
- Executive Summary
- Original Requirements
- Confirmed Offer Terms
- Compensation Scenarios
- Role and Career Tradeoffs
- Risks and Unknowns
- Reference and Background-check Plan
- Negotiation Options
- Decision Alternatives
- Questions to Verify
- Recommended Next Action
- Sources

Separate guaranteed/conditional/uncertain compensation. All calculation assumptions/currencies/
dates are explicit; do not provide invented tax or equity values. User chooses ACCEPT/DECLINE/
NEGOTIATE/DEFER and actual action is distinct from recommendation. Use
[template](../../templates/artifacts/offer-brief.md). Important statements classified and sourced.
